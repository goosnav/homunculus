# After hours

The user wants 10–30 minutes of interesting play and reasons to return, with creative direction delegated. The fixed observation-room camera, strange independent resident, Windows XP enclosure, and physical consequences remain. This is an expansion of the static JavaScript/Three.js game, not a new platform. No additional inference or asset downloads.

Direction: an inhabited service room in a building that denies having tenants. Worn, funny, unsettling. Lore appears as deliveries, a telephone, indoor weather, a loose shadow and a physical inspection seal. No quest list, currencies, achievement screen, model explanation or exposition wall.

A first after-hours shift has six encounters: an unsolicited parcel; rain, telephone and shadow encounters in a shuffled order; an inspector; an eviction notice. Between encounters there are 95–115 seconds of ordinary life, approach cues and repeatable tactile play. After a brief respite, Call night service can summon the next incident early. Each encounter has staged action sequences and multiple resolutions. The intended exploratory session is 10–30 minutes. No minimum-duration assertion substitutes for engagement: a knowledgeable active route can finish faster. Distinct encounters, alternate resolutions, side play and both endings are verified; enjoyment and retention require human playtesting.

Always available side play: plant and tend a seed, wind a clockwork mouse that the resident follows, feed a neighbor through the wall, float a paper boat. These use finite physical props, autonomous movement/work and reusable effects. Plants grow through light/water rather than one click. Overwatering makes puddles; draining recovers. The neighbor answers through knocks and returns small objects. The resident can refuse requests after cruelty; soup remains recovery.

Encounters:
- Parcel: rattles under the bed, grows legs. Open it (a tooth) or return it (a button and a receipt from tomorrow). Occupant physically handles it; choice changes the shelf keepsake.
- Indoor weather: a cloud leaks through a ceiling pipe. Grow the plant to a flower using water/light, or make a boat and drain the room after it has sailed. Water rises visibly but is recoverable; no untelegraphed instant death. Warm/cold weather varies each visit.
- Telephone: a ringing desk telephone and three lamps. Answer; its lamps reveal a three-number station sequence. Tune and send each tone, or hang up and feed the wall to have the neighbor answer. Wrong tones reset the attempt, repeatable clue. Physical reel or shell remains. No generated dialogue is replaced with canned speech.
- Borrowed shadow: resident loses his floor shadow; it climbs the wall. Wind the mouse to lure it onto the floor, then switch off the lights near the toy to catch it in a jar; alternatively open the shutter and ring the bell to let it leave. One reusable silhouette, no new render target.
- Inspection: a large mechanical eye with a stamp descends. A pictogram requests darkness/closed shutter or light/open shutter (visit-dependent). Hold the requested state for the inspection, or install the mirror and ring the bell to challenge it. Returns are harmless and retryable; no random irreversible soft lock.
- Eviction: the building releases its red door seal and the telephone prints VACANT while he is visibly present. Existing chalk/key/computer exit mechanisms become available now. Or press Refuse eviction: he bolts the door, takes the room number off and stays. Further encounters continue in the same life, without another forced finale.

Existing escapes remain physical, but the building's seal prevents a one-minute ending. Before the eviction the receiver says WAIT; release gives a concrete short denial. Death remains terminal at every stage. Leaving shows only the opening and empty room, never a destination. Death/forced reading/confinement cancel work without teleportation or late-result resurrection. Lights/radio/shutter remain usable while projects run; conflicting work is rejected rather than queued.

Persistence: retain existing bounded traces. Add optional sanitized afterHours record with capped completed-shift count, last choice and a bounded allowlist of physical keepsakes. No names, conversation, living state, queue or timestamps saved. Returning occupants encounter previous objects, different encounter order/codes/weather/inspection instructions. A denied or corrupt store cannot prevent play.

Technical: NightShift owns narrative state and side activities, composes with Chamber jobs (single movement owner). It exposes snapshot and context cue, accepts validated interactions, and runs before the chamber job step. NightView owns reusable props/animation and hit targets. All new events stay within existing event queue/memory/token caps. Context 512, input 256, single inference worker, male filtered voice unchanged. Framebuffer and geometry budgets stay bounded across lives and a 30-minute simulation. Use procedural low-poly props with existing materials; no huge texture or model expansion.

Acceptance: multiple public-button routes through a full shift, alternate outcomes, repeated-cycle timing, no soft locks after interruptions, physical prop screenshots, fixed camera, cache/voice/real inference, phone layout and static build, real browser soak and fresh whole-branch review. Publish tested changes to main under standing authorization. Physical iPhone and human 10–30 minute retention cannot be claimed from automation.
