The WASI browser adapter is `@bjorn3/browser_wasi_shim` 0.4.2 under the MIT license, included in `dist/licenses/browser-wasi-shim.txt`. Both packages and their integrity hashes are pinned in `package-lock.json`. Their source repositories are https://github.com/festvox/flite, https://github.com/echogarden-project/flite-wasi, and https://github.com/bjorn3/browser_wasi_shim.
