About this little existence

Homunculus 0.2

A little life behind glass.

He walks, rests, reads, gets ideas, and tests the door. His body runs on a state machine. His words and moods come from a small language model running on this computer.

Talk to him, drag him, tap the glass, or just watch. He can tell when you are looking. There is no score and you do not need to keep him alive. He remembers a little. He suspects a lot.

A fictional character built with Goosnav’s 3D assets. Nothing leaves your machine.
