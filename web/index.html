About this little existence

Homunculus 0.1

A little life behind glass.

He walks, rests, reads, gets ideas, and tests the door. His body runs on a state machine. His words and intentions come from a small local language model.

You can talk to him, move him, or just watch. There is no score. You don’t need to keep him alive. He remembers a little. He suspects a lot.

An illusion of a living character, built with Goosnav’s actual 3D assets. No cloud connection is needed after setup.

In appliance mode, the window is observation only. Configure it on the host before launching with --kiosk.
